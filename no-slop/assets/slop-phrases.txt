<!--
  slop-phrases.txt -- Asset for no-slop.

  Banned/cliche phrase list for scripts/slop-scan.sh. One
  case-insensitive fixed-string pattern per line.

  Scoring modes (understood by slop-scan.sh):
    # @shots               -- following patterns score 2 points per matched line
    # @density:<group>      -- following patterns are pooled per <group> and
                              scored by density per 1000 words:
                              >=12 -> +8, >=6 -> +5, >=2 -> +2, else +0.
    # @cluster:<group>      -- following patterns are checked per paragraph:
                              2+ DISTINCT patterns of the group in one
                              paragraph scores +3 (Tier 2 cluster rule).
  Lines starting with # (other than the markers above), blank lines, and
  the < > comment lines of this header are ignored.

  Word tiers: the @shots additions marked Tier 1A/1B, the @cluster:tier2
  group, and the @density:vague-praise / @density:boilerplate groups form
  a tiered vocabulary system. Tier 1A = AI frequency markers (flag on
  sight); Tier 1B = clarity edits (always worth fixing, not authorship
  evidence); Tier 2 = fine alone, flag in clusters; Tier 3 = flag by
  density only.

  Extend when new markers are found. Prefer empirically grounded additions:
  Kobak et al., "Delving into LLM-assisted writing in biomedical
  publications through excess vocabulary", Science Advances 11, eadt3813
  (2025) -- 15M+ PubMed abstracts; excess style words (delve, tapestry,
  underscore, meticulous, intricate) spiked post-ChatGPT. Note the drift
  caveat: the human baseline is moving toward AI vocabulary, so single-word
  tells decay over time -- structure, stance, and specificity are the
  durable signals, word lists are only the floor.
-->
