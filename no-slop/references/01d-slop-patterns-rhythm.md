# Slop patterns -- rhythm by rule, inflation extended

Shapes and punctuation applied everywhere, whether or not the meaning
asks for them; ordinary facts dressed as pivotal or expert-backed.

## Forced triads

- **Pattern:** ideas arriving in threes to sound complete whether the
  meaning has three parts or not ("innovation, inspiration, and
  insights"; three parallel examples plus a lesson).
- **Why it reads as AI:** the count serves the rhythm, not the meaning.
  Use the number of items the meaning needs; develop the strongest one.
- **Rule:** Q-02.

## Repeated sentence openings

- **Pattern:** several sentences in a row starting with the same
  subject ("She noted... She noted... She filed...").
- **Why it reads as AI:** repetition handled by rule instead of by ear.
  Merge the sentences, change the subject, or begin with the action.
- **Rule:** Q-02.

## Passive voice and missing subjects

- **Pattern:** "No configuration file needed."; "The results are
  preserved automatically."; "Mistakes were made."
- **Why it reads as AI:** hides who acts or drops the subject. Name the
  actor when that helps: "You do not need a configuration file."
- **Rule:** Q-01.

## Stacked qualifiers

- **Pattern:** "could potentially possibly be argued"; "may eventually
  unlock"; "might ultimately transform".
- **Why it reads as AI:** each hedge cancels the next, leaving a
  sentence that asserts nothing while sounding cautious. Keep the one
  qualifier the source supports.
- **Rule:** P-03.

## Vague connection

- **Pattern:** "associated with", "in connection with", "linked to",
  "tied to" -- without saying how.
- **Why it reads as AI:** "He was associated with the leadership of
  ExampleCorp" hides whether he was CEO, board member, or consultant.
  Name the relationship the source gives; if the source does not say,
  keep the vague wording rather than inventing a role.
- **Rule:** H-01.

## Shallow -ing riders

- **Pattern:** "highlighting...", "underscoring...", "reflecting...",
  "showcasing..." bolted onto a simple fact to sound deeper.
- **Why it reads as AI:** the rider pretends to explain meaning. Keep
  the fact; keep the rider only when the source supports what it claims.
- **Before:** "The launch adds file search, highlighting the team's
  commitment to better workflows."
- **After:** "The launch adds file search."
- **Rule:** Q-01.

## Sales language

- **Pattern:** "nestled within the breathtaking region";
  "a vibrant hub of innovation"; "must-visit"; "stunning".
- **Why it reads as AI:** the text reads like an advertisement. State
  what the thing is.
- **Before:** "Nestled within the breathtaking region of Gonder,
  Alamata Raya Kobo stands as a vibrant town."
- **After:** "Alamata Raya Kobo is a town in the Gonder region."
- **Rule:** Q-05.

## Borrowed authority, extended

- **Pattern:** prestige-outlet lists ("cited in NYT, BBC, FT, and The
  Hindu"); follower counts ("over 500,000 followers"); unnamed
  "industry reports".
- **Why it reads as AI:** a name or number stands in for what was said.
  Name a real source and what it said, or remove the claim or list.
- **Rule:** H-01, H-04.

## Copula avoidance

- **Pattern:** "serves as", "stands as", "functions as", "boasts",
  "features", "offers" where "is", "are", or "has" would do.
- **Why it reads as AI:** simple verbs replaced with longer phrases.
  "Gallery 825 serves as the exhibition space" -> "Gallery 825 is the
  exhibition space."
- **Rule:** Q-01.
